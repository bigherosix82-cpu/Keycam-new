FAQ

How do I order?

Choose a design, open its product page, select the product type, size and thickness, add it to My Order, then send the order through Telegram.

Can I order more than one design?

Yes. Add multiple designs and different variants to My Order. One Telegram message will contain the full order.

Can I choose FPS or Glass for any design?

Yes. Every design uses one unified product page where Mouse Pad, FPS Mouse Pad and Glass options can be selected before choosing the available size and thickness.

Do you deliver in Cambodia?

KeyCam focuses on Cambodia delivery. Delivery details are confirmed directly on Telegram.

Are the prices discounted?

No. The website shows everyday/original prices only. Event promotions are separate.
