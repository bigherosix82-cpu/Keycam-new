PRODUCT SPECS

Mouse pad details.

Compare the materials, glide, use case and key advantages of all three mouse-pad types.

REGULAR MOUSE PAD

Standard / Office

MATERIALUsually cloth surfaces with a rubber or foam base.
GLIDE & CONTROLBalanced, standard glide with moderate friction for everyday tracking accuracy.
BEST FORGeneral daily computing, office work, casual browsing, and low-intensity tasks.
KEY ADVANTAGEAffordable, comfortable on the wrist, versatile, and works seamlessly with almost any standard optical mouse.
COLOR ACCURACYFinal printed colors on fabric may vary slightly (roughly 10%) from the digital screen due to the sublimation heating process.
FPS MOUSE PAD

Gaming Cloth / Hybrid

MATERIALSpecialized textured cloth, synthetic weaves, or hybrid surfaces optimized for speed or control.
GLIDE & CONTROLEngineered specifically for fast-paced gaming; often categorized into "speed" pads (low friction for quick flicks) or "control" pads (higher friction for precise stopping power).
BEST FORFirst-person shooter (FPS) games and competitive gaming where quick reflexes and micro-adjustments matter.
KEY ADVANTAGEOffers consistent, reliable stopping power and tracking precision tailored to high-DPI or low-sens gaming styles.
COLOR ACCURACYFinal printed colors on fabric may vary slightly (roughly 10%) from the digital screen due to the sublimation heating process.
GLASS MOUSEPAD

Hard Surface

MATERIALTempered glass with a micro-textured or frosted coating.
GLIDE & CONTROLExtremely fast and ultra-smooth glide with almost zero initial friction.
BEST FORHardcore gamers, tracking-heavy tracking genres, and users who prefer a slick, zero-drag feel.
KEY ADVANTAGEUnmatched durability (virtually wear-proof), incredibly easy to clean, and provides a completely uniform glide across the entire surface. However, they are fragile if dropped, can feel cold to the touch, and wear down mouse feet faster.
COLOR ACCURACYFinal printed colors may vary slightly from the digital screen.
