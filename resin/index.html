FAQ

Good to know

How long does it take?

Every keycap is made to order. Allow 2–3 days for us to pour, seal and finish your keys before they're ready.

Which row is my key?

Cherry is sculpted per row, so the row has to match the key you're replacing.

R1 — Esc and the number row (top). R2 — QWERTY row. R3 — the home row (ASDF). R4 — bottom letter row (ZXCV).

Not sure? Send us a photo of your keyboard on Telegram and we'll tell you which row you need.

Which profiles can I pick?

Cherry in R1, R2, R3, R4 and a Cherry space bar, or SA in R1 only — SA R2, R3 and R4 are not available.

Can I mix different logos in one order?

Yes, any mix. Four keys can be four different designs. The 4+ price is counted per profile, so it applies to the total number of Cherry keys and separately to the total number of SA keys.

How do the 4+ prices work?

4 or more keys of the same profile unlock the lower price for every key of that profile.

Example: 4 Cherry keys = $10 total. 4 SA keys = $16 total. The space bar is always $10.

How do I order my own logo?

Open the Your own logo collection, add the keys you need, then send the file to us on Telegram. A PNG with a transparent background works best, at least 1000px on the longest side.

Does the space bar come with a logo?

It's optional. Pick a design for it or leave it plain — the price is $10 either way.
